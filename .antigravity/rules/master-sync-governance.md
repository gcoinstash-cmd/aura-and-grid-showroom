**Scope**: Workspace Governance Rule — Aura & Grid Storefront & Vault Synchronization  
**Enforcement**: Mandatory Operational Mandates for Lead Systems Engineer & Crew Chief / All Operational Sessions

---

## 1. ATOMIC MASTER SYNC
- **Synchronous Dual-Record Update Requirement**: On every cluster synthesis or archive event, the agent MUST immediately update both:
  1. `docs/deal-room/MICRO_CLUSTERS_COMMERCIAL_MANIFEST.md` (recording archive bundle path, MD5 checksum, byte size, triad breakdown, architectural domain, dyno verification, and benchmark replacement value).
  2. `ACTIVE_STATE.md` (synchronizing total units tracked, sealed cluster milestones, and active git commit telemetry).
- **Atomic Execution Invariant**: NEVER stage, commit, or close a cluster archive session without updating both master synchronization records (`MICRO_CLUSTERS_COMMERCIAL_MANIFEST.md` and `ACTIVE_STATE.md`) in the exact same operational run.

## 2. BOUNDARY FIREWALL
- **Public Showroom Capping**:
  - Public catalog files and compiled showroom assets (`site/index.html`, `scripts/site/build_commercial_showroom.mjs`, and root index) MUST remain strictly capped at **5 production clusters** (`MC-01` through `MC-05`, 17 Triads / 51 Units, **$805,000 USD** portfolio APA baseline).
  - Additional vaulted clusters (Cluster 06, Cluster 07, and subsequent expansions) are categorized strictly as **Vault Expansion Archives** / offline cold-storage assets and must never be injected into public showroom catalog builds.
- **Private Collection Shielding**:
  - Strictly isolate internal private collection assets, private engines, and console dashboards from public manifests and commercial repositories.
  - Zero exposure of internal unit serials (`#338`–`#354`) or internal registry prefixes (`GF-T1`, `GF-T2`, `GF-T3`, `T1-`/`T2-`/`T3-`) on public showroom or deal-room assets; use public identifiers (`MC-01` through `MC-05`) only.
  - Strictly enforce zero-leak vocabulary bans: Zero exposure of internal development engine names (`"Ghost Factory OS"` / `"GhostFactoryOS"`), and zero unapproved buzzwords (`"AI"`, `"LLM"`, `"autonomous"`, `"automated"`, `"agents"`) in public showroom and deal-room collateral.

## 3. COMMIT TELEMETRY
- **End-of-Batch Push Requirement**: Every manifest update and state synchronization must conclude with a clean git commit and push to `origin/main`.
- **Dual-Remote Upstream Synchronization**: Telemetry pushes must synchronize across configured remote mirrors (`origin/main`), ensuring local and remote repositories remain fully aligned.
- **Audit Verification**: Ensure `ACTIVE_STATE.md` records the active commit telemetry and dyno test verification status (`105/105 PASS`).
