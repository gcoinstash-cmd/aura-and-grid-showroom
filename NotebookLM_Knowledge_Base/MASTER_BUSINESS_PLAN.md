## 5. Micro-APA Governance & Transaction Boundaries

Every micro-APA executed by Aura & Grid must incorporate:
1. **Defined Asset Schedule**: Explicitly list code repository, documentation, and specific digital assets transferred.
2. **Explicit Shared IP Carve-Outs**: Permanent exclusion of GhostFactoryOS core, Aura & Grid brands, shared UI components, design tokens, build scripts, and future catalog releases.
3. **No Portfolio Liquidation**: The transaction conveys rights to one defined asset, never the factory or dealership.
4. **IP Chain-of-Title Review**: Verification of clean authorship and permissive MIT/permissive dependency licensing.
5. **Simulated Data Disclosures**: Explicit contractual warranty that the asset is a prototype/simulation operating on sample data and does not represent an audited production service.

## 6. Product Truth Labels & Diligence Standards

All marketing copy, sales offers, and technical data sheets must strictly employ authorized product truth labels:

- **Authorized Labels**:
  - `Template`: Source-code starting point for customization.
  - `Interactive Prototype`: Clickable sample workflow without claim of live operations.
  - `Hosted Demo`: Publicly accessible preview utilizing sample or simulated data.
  - `Simulation`: Illustrative sample data, physics calculations, or events; not live data.
  - `Deployable Source Template`: Code, database schema, seed data, and setup guides ready for deployment.
  - `Customer-Configured Deployment`: Tailored and verified within a client's specific cloud environment.
  - `Production Service`: Verified real users, security configurations, automated backups, and live operational support.

- **Prohibited Claims (Unless deterministically proven)**:
  - "Production-ready", "Enterprise-grade", "Full compliance", "Real-time", "Verified", "Secure", "Audited", "WCAG compliant", "HIPAA-ready", "Financial-grade", "Flight-qualified", "Space-qualified", "Safety-certified".
