# ENGINE_SPEC.md — APEX-205 Liquidity Sweep & Smart Order Routing (SOR) Architecture ## 1. Architectural Topology & System Boundaries The **APEX-205 SOR Engine** is a deterministic institutional liquidity aggregation and execution routing framework. It connects direct market access (DMA) lit pools, dark liquidity crossing networks, retail ECNs, and institutional block-cross networks. - **Ingestion Tier**: High-throughput REST & WebSocket order gateways supporting sub-millisecond parsing and validation. - **Optimization Core**: Karush-Kuhn-Tucker (KKT) convex quadratic programming allocation engine solving multi-venue order splitting under depth constraints and nonlinear market impact functions. - **Ledger Guarantee**: Cryptographic append-only SHA-256 chain ensuring tamper-proof audit trails for every order execution. --- ## 2. Proprietary Mathematical & Algorithmic Formulation ### Nonlinear Quadratic Market Impact Model $$\text{Impact}_i(x_i) = \gamma \cdot P_i \cdot \left(\frac{x_i}{\text{Depth}_i}\right)^{1.5}$$ Where: - $x_i$ = child order allocation size sent to venue $i$ (in Units). - $\text{Depth}_i$ = visible liquidity depth at venue $i$. - $P_i$ = base quote price at venue $i$. - $\gamma$ = market volatility impact coefficient (default $0.15$). ### Latency-Adjusted Fill Probability $$P(\text{Fill})_i = \exp\left(-\lambda \cdot \frac{\text{Latency}_i}{10}\right) \cdot \text{Reliability}_i$$ Where $\lambda$ is the latency sensitivity penalty parameter. ### KKT Objective Function Minimize total execution cost subject to sum conservation: $$\min_{\{x_i\}} \sum_{i} \left[ x_i \cdot P_i + x_i \cdot \text{Impact}_i(x_i) + x_i \cdot \frac{\text{Fee}_i}{10000} \cdot P_i \right]$$ $$\text{Subject to } \sum_{i} x_i = X_{\text{parent}}, \quad 0 \le x_i \le \text{Depth}_i$$ --- ## 3.
