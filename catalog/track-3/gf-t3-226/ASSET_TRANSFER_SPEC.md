Every commit to the `GF-T3-226` repository must carry cryptographic GPG/SSH signatures, author identity attestation, timestamped diff provenance, and linked verification test logs to guarantee full regulatory compliance and forensic traceability.

### B. Automated Security Scanning for IaC & Container Images
