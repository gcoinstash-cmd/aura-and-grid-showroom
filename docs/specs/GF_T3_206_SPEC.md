Proprietary Mathematical & Algorithmic Engine ### A. Dynamic Hedge Ratio (Engle-Granger OLS) Given two synthetic clusters $X_t$ and $Y_t$, the instantaneous hedge ratio $\beta_t$ and intercept $\alpha_t$ are continuously estimated via rolling Ordinary Least Squares over a fixed window $N$: $$Y_t = \beta X_t + \alpha + \epsilon_t$$ $$\beta = \frac{\sum (X_t - \bar{X})(Y_t - \bar{Y})}{\sum (X_t - \bar{X})^2}$$ ### B. Ornstein-Uhlenbeck SDE Calibration The residual spread $S_t = Y_t - (\beta X_t + \alpha)$ is modeled as a continuous Ornstein-Uhlenbeck stochastic differential equation: $$dS_t = \theta (\mu - S_t)dt + \sigma dW_t$$ Where: - $\theta$ is the speed of mean reversion. - $\mu$ is the equilibrium mean level. - $\sigma$ is the instantaneous volatility. - $dW_t$ is a standard Wiener process. The analytical mean-reversion **Half-Life** $H$ is computed as: $$H = \frac{\ln(2)}{\theta}$$ ### C. Augmented Dickey-Fuller Stationarity Test To guarantee cointegration validity, APEX-206 tests residual stationarity against the null hypothesis of a unit root at 95% ($p < 0.05$) and 99% ($p < 0.01$) confidence thresholds. --- ## 3.
