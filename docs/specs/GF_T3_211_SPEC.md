# GF-T3-211: APEX-211 Cross-Asset Cointegration & Copula Stat-Arb Engine ## Enterprise Reference Core Specification ### 1. Architectural Topology & System Overview The APEX-211 statistical arbitrage engine is an institutional-grade cross-asset quantitative trading and cointegration system designed for high-frequency state-space execution. - **Core Processing Pipeline**: Zero-lookahead Kalman filter hedge ratio adaptation combined with Engle-Granger and Johansen cointegration checks. - **Tail Risk Copula Engine**: Bivariate Clayton and Gumbel copulas capturing asymmetric lower-tail market crashes and upper-tail blow-offs. - **Cryptographic Immutability**: Append-only SHA-256 state ledger hashing every filter update, copula recalibration, and execution signal. ### 2. Mathematical & Algorithmic Formulations #### A. Dynamic Kalman Filter State Space Model Let the observation equation relating asset Y_t and asset X_t be: Y_t = beta_t * X_t + alpha_t + v_t, v_t ~ N(0, R) State transition equation: [beta_t, alpha_t]^T = [beta_{t-1}, alpha_{t-1}]^T + w_t, w_t ~ N(0, W) Where W = diag(delta, delta) is the state noise covariance matrix, and R is the observation variance. #### B. Ornstein-Uhlenbeck Mean Reversion & Half-Life Spread dynamics are modeled as an Ornstein-Uhlenbeck (OU) stochastic differential equation: dX_t = theta * (mu - X_t) * dt + sigma * dW_t The analytical half-life of mean reversion t_{1/2} is derived as: t_{1/2} = ln(2) / theta #### C. Bivariate Copulas for Tail Risk - **Clayton Copula** (Lower tail dependence for joint drawdowns): C_alpha(u, v) = (u^{-alpha} + v^{-alpha} - 1)^{-1/alpha}, alpha in (0, infinity) Lower tail dependence parameter: lambda_L = 2^{-1/alpha} - **Gumbel Copula** (Upper tail dependence for joint rallies): C_theta(u, v) = exp( - ( (-ln u)^theta + (-ln v)^theta )^{1/theta} ), theta in [1, infinity) Upper tail dependence parameter: lambda_U = 2 - 2^{1/theta} ### 3.
