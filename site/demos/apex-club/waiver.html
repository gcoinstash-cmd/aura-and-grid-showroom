Sparring & Contact Waiver

All athletes entering the ring, sparring circle, or heavy bag line must execute this legal release.

Section 1: Inherent Risks of Combat Sports

I acknowledge and fully understand that martial arts, boxing, kickboxing, and sparring involve intense physical contact, high-impact exertion, and inherent risks of injury, including but not limited to concussions, sprains, and fractures.

Section 2: Protective Gear Mandate

I certify that I will at all times during sparring sessions utilize approved headgear, custom fitted mouthguards, 16oz boxing gloves, and groin protection inspected by an APEX coach.

Section 3: Voluntary Release of Liability

I freely and voluntarily assume all risks associated with participation and discharge APEX CLUB, coaches, and staff from any and all liability or claims.
